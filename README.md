# Free Tools Directory

A curated index of free browser-based tools for small businesses, creators, marketers, and developers.

Most tools in this directory are privacy-friendly, no-signup utilities that run in the browser or provide fast reference workflows. The collection is maintained by CoolDK Studio.

## Business And Documents

### Business Template Hub

[Business Template Hub](https://templatehub.app) provides free business document templates with no signup. It focuses on SOPs, job descriptions, and other small-business documents with clean exports for Word, Google Docs, and PDF.

### Invoice PDF

[Invoice PDF](https://invoicepdf.app) creates professional invoices, receipts, and estimates as PDFs in seconds. It is built for privacy-first browser workflows with no signup, no watermark, and no upload.

### B2B ROI Calculator

[B2B ROI Calculator](https://b2broicalculator.com) helps teams model marketing, SEO, CRM, and B2B growth ROI with benchmarks, transparent formulas, and exportable reports.

### Free Contract Maker

[Free Contract Maker](https://freecontractmaker.app) helps small businesses and freelancers draft common agreements through guided questions and export editable Word or PDF documents.


### Plainrow

[Plainrow](https://fromtill.com/plainrow/) stacks and dedupes CSV sheets in the browser with nothing uploaded. Lite is free forever for two-file stack and dedupe; when a client catalog needs a warehouse left-join (or split/clean), Kitchen is a $19 one-time zip that still runs on this computer.

## AI, SEO, And Productivity

### AI SEO Hub

[AI SEO Hub](https://aiseohub.app) offers free browser-based SEO utilities for meta descriptions, title tags, schema, keyword ideas, and sitemaps.

### Flow Templates

[Flow Templates](https://flowtemplates.app) is a library of workflow automation templates for n8n, Zapier, Make, and related business automation tools.

### Prompt Library

[Prompt Library](https://getpromptlibrary.com) is a clean collection of AI prompts for business, creative, writing, and productivity tasks across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and other AI tools.

### MinutesAI

[MinutesAI](https://getminutesai.com) turns pasted meeting, call, video, or webinar transcripts into structured summaries, decisions, and action items without adding a bot to calls.

## Developer Tools

### Diagramgen

[Diagramgen](https://diagramgen.app) creates flowcharts, sequence diagrams, ER diagrams, UML diagrams, mind maps, and architecture diagrams from text.

### AI Coding Hub

[AI Coding Hub](https://aicodinghub.app) helps developers compare AI coding assistants, vibe coding tools, model workflows, and AI development stacks.

## Creator Tools

### Free Mockup

[Free Mockup](https://freemockup.app) is a browser-based mockup generator for T-shirts, books, wall art, print-on-demand assets, and creator workflows.

### Free Video Compressor

[Free Video Compressor](https://freevideocompressor.app) compresses MP4 and other video files locally in the browser, with no upload, no signup, and no watermark.

### Free Tattoo

[Free Tattoo](https://freetattoo.app) helps people explore AI-assisted tattoo ideas, prompt presets, style directions, placement concepts, and stencil-friendly inspiration.

### Niche Logo

[Niche Logo](https://nichelogo.app) creates niche logo concepts for bands, gaming, sports teams, creators, YouTube channels, and small brands, with SVG or PNG exports.

### Free Room Redesign

[Free Room Redesign](https://freeroomredesign.com) helps homeowners and creators explore AI room design ideas, before-after examples, and room-specific style directions.

### Free Headshot

[Free Headshot](https://freeheadshot.app) helps users compare professional AI headshot styles for LinkedIn, resumes, team pages, and personal branding.

## Suggested Starting Points

- Need a business document: start with Business Template Hub, Invoice PDF, or Free Contract Maker.
- Need offline CSV stack/dedupe without uploading: start with Plainrow.
- Need SEO or AI workflow help: start with AI SEO Hub, Prompt Library, Flow Templates, or MinutesAI.
- Need developer documentation visuals: start with Diagramgen.
- Need creative assets: start with Free Mockup, Niche Logo, Free Tattoo, Free Room Redesign, or Free Headshot.
